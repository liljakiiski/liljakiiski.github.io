Maze Traversing Robot


Python Linux Raspberry Pi Electrical Robotics LEGO

Overview

As the final project for ENGR162 my team was tasked to create a LEGO robot to autonomously navigate a walled maze, while avoiding hazards (magnetic and IR beacons). My team created a robot using three ultrasonic sensors (at the front, and left/right sides), alongside an IMU and IR sensor for hazard detection. I also worked on developing and fine-tuning a PID algorithm to optimize the robot’s turning and staying on course. I also worked on creating a custom cargo container with CAD.

Project Technical Presentation




Conclusions

Ultimately, our robot managed to complete around 3/4ths of the maze, and was able to detect all obstacles. It accomplished most project objectives, and proved its functionality in driving, turning, cargo holding and deployment, and autonomous navigation.

However, the robot ultimately struggled with precise turning, and the main problem I found during the final demonstration was that of error accumulation over large distances. To improve, more fine precision in turning should be worked on.
