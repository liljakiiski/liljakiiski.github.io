Schoology Classmates

August 2024
JavaScript HTML/CSS Chrome-Extention

Schoology was my high school’s learning and grading system, which included a feature to view your classmates in a specific class you were in. This feature was later hidden by the system, however I developed a chrome extension to bring back the functionality, in essence to view the hidden profiles.

PID Simulator

July - August 2022
Java Simulator
I was responsible for teaching PID (Proportional Integral Derivative Feedback Control) for the new members of my high school FIRST Robotics Team. I found when learning about PID and its applications, visuals helped me a lot, thus I thought it helpful to develop a basic simulator for PID and the different input values
