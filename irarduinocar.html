IR Remote Controlled Car


C++ Arduino Robotics Electrical

Overview

I programmed a custom Arduino-based driving system for a miniature robot car, to drive left/right/forwards/backwards based on input from an IR transmitter. I also implemented PID control to smoothen the robot’s driving. The project used an Arduino MEGA, a DC motor controller, and IR receiver and transmitter, and assorted wires.
